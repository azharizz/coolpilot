# COOLPilot

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React required by the user for a web interface. Python/OpenCV benchmark backend, SQLite, native ARM Docker. Backend choices delegated by the implementation request.

## Users

Computer-vision engineers evaluating camera-health evidence and bounded configurations for ADAS research. Reviewers need inspectable timing, quality and failure evidence.

## Product Purpose

Inspect three recorded camera views, report separate delivery and image-quality evidence, and validate camera-health configurations against frozen gates.

## Operating Context

Single operator on local ARM first. Licensed AWS COOL/Graviton is a separate deployment prerequisite, not enabled by a standard OpenCV upgrade. The app is camera-only; mouse-pad tracking has been retired.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Timestamp-paced camera replay, optional synthetic fault injection and bounded configuration search. The Oxford fixture is development-only and cannot authorize apply. Nebius and optional Decisions credentials remain server-side. Visible measurements must come from actual execution. Local OpenCV cannot be labelled licensed COOL or Graviton. No automatic camera control, driving decisions or safety certification. Work solo. No em dashes.

## Product Principles

Evidence before recommendations. Quality gates are explicit. Rejected experiments remain inspectable. One click starts the real pipeline; advanced controls remain optional. Results precede supporting detail. Failure is visible and recoverable.

## Brand Commitments

Name: COOLPilot. OpenCV-inspired black, white and RGB visual system with Poppins. Compact, plain product language. Desktop is the current priority.
